## Objective

This project explored how complementary biometric signals can be combined for identification. It brought together **RGB face images, thermal face images, and voice recordings** within a multimodal system.

## Approach

The implementation used **Local Ternary Patterns** for image features and **Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients** for audio features. The extracted representations were combined to investigate feature fusion across the visual and audio modalities.

## Practical focus

A central part of the work was preparing and aligning features from different input types. Image and audio preprocessing, representation design, and feature alignment were essential to combining the modalities within a consistent evaluation pipeline.
