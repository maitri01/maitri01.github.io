## Problem

Optimal transport provides a way to compare distributions, but its computational cost can limit practical use as datasets grow. My undergraduate research at NMIMS University, under the mentorship of Dr. Sharath Raghvendra, focused on improving the efficiency of Wasserstein distance computation.

## My contribution

I worked on a **Java implementation using quad-tree methods**, profiling the computation and improving how spatial structure was used during processing.

The work combined algorithm analysis with implementation: identifying bottlenecks, revising data structures, and evaluating the effect of those changes on execution.

## Research perspective

The work focused on the relationship between spatial structure and computational cost, using profiling to guide implementation changes and assess their effect on performance.
