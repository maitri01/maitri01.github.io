## Objective

This project explored the use of an LSTM model for intraday stock modelling, using historical price sequences and engineered technical indicators.

## Approach

The implementation used minute-level **Apple stock data from Yahoo Finance**. Features included moving averages, momentum, volatility, and time-series forecasts, arranged into sequences for model training and prediction.

## Technical focus

The work involved preparing sequential data, engineering financial time-series features, and tuning a recurrent model. Evaluation focused on interpreting prediction results in the presence of noisy observations and the risk of overfitting.
